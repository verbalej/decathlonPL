    Stay safe in the Tatras

    Check the forecast — and the avalanche level

    Conditions above 1,500 m can be wintry from October. Check TOPR's reports before you go and turn back if the weather turns.

    Start early

    Days are short in autumn. Plan to be off exposed ridges well before sunset, and always carry a headlamp.

    Know who to call

    TOPR mountain rescue: 985 or +48 601 100 300. The RATUNEK app sends your GPS position to rescuers.
